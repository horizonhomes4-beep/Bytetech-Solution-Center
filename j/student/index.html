Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in a full mock?

A full mock follows the JAMB four-subject structure: 60 Use of English questions and 40 questions for each of three other subjects, making 180 questions.

Can I practise only one subject?

Yes. Subject mocks can be taken independently and are scored separately.

Can I print my result?

Yes. Completed results include a print/save-as-PDF option where enabled by the platform.

What happens if my internet connection fails?

MathCloud displays a clear offline screen when your device loses network access. Live Firebase question loading, saving and result submission require reconnection.

Does MathCloud replace the official JAMB examination?

No. MathCloud is a preparation platform. Scores are estimates for practice and are not official JAMB results.
